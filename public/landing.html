FAQ

Common questions

How does the AI recommendation engine work?▼
We use Claude by Anthropic to analyze your real revenue and activity data. It generates personalized insights covering revenue trends, anomalies, growth opportunities, team performance, and specific action items.
Can I export my data at any time?▼
Yes. Export your analytics as PDF, Excel (XLSX), CSV, or JSON at any time. The export page lets you choose your dataset and date range.
How does multi-tenant access work?▼
Each workspace is fully isolated. Invite team members and assign roles: Owner, Admin, Member, or Viewer. Each role has specific permissions for what they can view or modify.
Is there a free trial for Pro?▼
Start on the Free plan immediately with no credit card required. When you're ready, upgrading to Pro unlocks AI insights, all export formats, and automated email reports.
What happens to my data if I cancel?▼
Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, giving you time to export everything. After that, it's permanently deleted from our servers.
